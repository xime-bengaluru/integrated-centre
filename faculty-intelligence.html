Intelligence
Single-point-of-failure alert
82% of EP revenue is generated by one faculty member. Dr. Bhatta's absence, overload, or departure would reduce XIME's external program income to near zero. This is not an MDP practice — it is one practitioner operating without an institutional support structure.
What works — the pattern
Every program that ran had pre-committed participants. Bhatta brings his own network. ACMA brings its members. Programs built on market assumptions cancelled every time.
What fails — the pattern
8 of 24 programs didn't run. All cancelled MDPs show zero revenue — designed from the faculty side, not the market side. No confirmed demand before the date was set.
The next three channel partners to activate
MMA Chennai — via Vijayakumar
Association channel. Pre-committed membership base. Same model as ACMA — demand comes with the partner.
KSUM Kerala
Startup ecosystem channel. Entrepreneurship and innovation programs. Kochi campus delivery advantage.
Electronic City corporates — direct
200+ IT companies within 5km. Mid-manager cohorts. Requires corporate sales capacity Bhatta should not carry alone.
The target for next year
If two new channel partners are activated and three existing faculty are brought into MDP delivery, Bhatta's contribution stays constant but drops from 82% to around 40% of a much larger total. That is what a sustainable EP P&L looks like.
