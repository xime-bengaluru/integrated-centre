Common Questions
Is my work saved automatically?
Yes. Your answers save as you type, and the bar at the top shows the time of the last save. You can also press Save draft any time. You can close this page and come back later, your text will be here.
Who will see what I write?
Your functional head, your campus Director, and the President. It is a simple yearly record so that your work is seen and recognised.
What if I do not have much to write in a box?
The suggested numbers under each box are a guide, not a rule. Write what is true for your role. If a box has little to add, a line is fine.
Something looks wrong or the page seems empty. What do I do?
Please do not retype everything. Refresh the page first. If it still looks wrong, contact Reetha at 9845280734 and your saved text will be recovered.
