Phase 1 — Initiation
0
Manpower Requisition Form Submitted
New Step
Department head submits a formal Manpower Requisition Form via HRMS. Role type (New / Backfill / Projected), justification, required qualifications, and proposed start date are captured at source.
Form Covers
  • Role title, department, role type (New / Backfill / Projected)
  • Justification for hire — business rationale required
  • Preferred qualification and experience range
  • Proposed joining date and reporting line
  • Salary band reference (existing bands to be referenced, not overridden)
⚠ Control Point — No recruitment triggered without approved form

Phase 5 — Selection & Offer · New Steps
✓
Approval Gate 3 — Final Selection Decision
Approval Required
Approver: President  |  SLA: 48 hours post final interview
President confirms selected candidate and triggers salary fitment check against existing approved bands. No offer extended without this confirmation.
HRMS Trigger — Selection confirmation prompt sent to President after final interview is marked complete
9
Salary Fitment Check
New Step
Candidate's salary expectation (collected via Candidate Information Form) checked against existing XIME salary bands for the role level. Fitment confirmed before offer is prepared.
Process Notes
  • Salary bands already exist — referenced here, not created afresh
  • If expectation exceeds band: exception approval required before proceeding
  • Fitment check recorded in HRMS against the candidate's file
⚠ Control Point — Offer letter not drafted until fitment confirmed
10
Offer Letter Preparation & Issuance
New Step
Recruitment Professional prepares offer letter from standardised template using fitment-confirmed figures. Routed for authorised signature before issuance.
Process Notes
  • Standard offer letter template — no ad-hoc drafting
  • Signatory authority: President for faculty; Dean / Designate for admin roles
  • Offer validity stated on letter (recommend 5–7 working days)
  • Verbal offer made first; written offer issued within 24 hours of verbal acceptance
HRMS Trigger — Offer letter routed to signatory for approval before sending to candidate
✓
Approval Gate 4 — Offer Letter Sign-off
Approval Required
Approver: President (faculty) / Designated Dean (admin)  |  SLA: 24 hours
Signed offer letter returned to Recruitment Professional for despatch to candidate.
HRMS Trigger — Digital sign-off routed in system; auto-despatch to candidate on approval
11
Candidate Response & Negotiation
New Step
Candidate accepts, negotiates, or declines within offer validity window. Acceptance confirmed in writing. Decline triggers return to next shortlisted candidate from Step 8 pool.
Process Notes
  • Negotiation not handled ad hoc — any deviation from offer escalated for approval
  • Decline reason captured in HRMS — useful for pipeline and market intelligence
HRMS Trigger — Acceptance confirmation recorded; pre-joining checklist auto-initiated

Framework Alignment — Why This Process Is Built the Way It Is

SHRM Talent Acquisition Framework
This process follows SHRM's four-stage model: Workforce Planning → Sourcing → Screening & Selection → Onboarding. The Manpower Sanction (Step 0) directly maps to SHRM's workforce planning gate — a requirement that is missing from most informal institutional processes.
ISO 9001 — Process Approach
ISO 9001 requires documented, repeatable processes with defined inputs, outputs, and control points. Every approval gate in this flow is an ISO control point. The SLA structure (48-hour response windows) is consistent with ISO's measurable process objectives requirement.
EFMD / APCSB Accreditation
EFMD Programme Accreditation and APCSB both evaluate institutional governance, including HR processes. Documented hiring procedures with approval chains, equity in sourcing (internal posting first), and record retention directly support XIME's 2030 accreditation goals. This process creates an auditable trail.
PDCA Cycle
Plan (Requisition + JD) → Do (Sourcing + Screening + Interviews) → Check (Approval gates + Fitment) → Act (Offer + Pre-joining + Onboarding). The weekly Saturday status report to leaders is the Check mechanism that feeds continuous improvement back into the process.
